\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

ASZ has three main limitations. First, reverse-greedy plan generation fixes each round once and therefore does not guarantee a globally optimal joint configuration. Broader joint or beam search could examine more configurations at additional preprocessing cost. Second, plan transferability depends on how similar the arrays are, even within one application. Consecutive snapshots, neighboring QMCPACK orbitals, and the horizontal Isabel wind components exchange plans with little loss, whereas transfers between the vertical and horizontal wind components lose up to about half of the compression ratio. Criteria that automatically decide which arrays can share a plan remain future work. Third, the implementation and evaluation target single-precision three-dimensional tensors, interpolation-based prediction, and serial CPU execution.
