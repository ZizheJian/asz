\begin{abstract}
Scientific simulations and instruments generate massive volumes of data. Error-bounded lossy compressors reduce these data volumes while controlling reconstruction errors. Within this class, prediction-based methods such as SZ3 and QoZ use lightweight operations to decorrelate data, achieving high compression ratios at high throughput. They also adapt their workflows to each input and error bound to improve these ratios. However, we find that further gains are possible while keeping total runtime practical.

In this paper, we develop ASZ by co-designing configurable compression mechanisms and their tuning workflow. ASZ introduces per-round prediction-axis choices, variable-coefficient predictors, and selectable quantization-bin serialization orders to improve compression ratios. To efficiently explore the resulting larger search space, ASZ combines reverse-greedy search with fast evaluation of candidate parameters.

Experiments on 12 scientific fields show that, at equal PSNR, ASZ achieves geometric-mean compression ratios of $2.68\times$ and $2.20\times$ those of SZ3 and QoZ, respectively. At a relative error bound of $10^{-3}$, ASZ reduces total codec time (including per-file preprocessing) by $13.7\%$ on average relative to QoZ. \textcolor{red}{The generated plan can often be reused across tens or even hundreds of related files, amortizing the tuning cost across them.}
\end{abstract}
